# Premium Creative Agency Website Research for the ScaleAcres Redesign

**Date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** 150 verified creative, branding, digital, motion, video and real-estate marketing agency websites worldwide, organised into seven study categories, plus a Top 20 shortlist.  
**Status:** Research only. No ScaleAcres code or design was touched.

## Files in this folder

- `agency-websites-master-list.csv` - all 150 verified sites with every research field.
- `TOP-20-SHORTLIST.md` - the 20 strongest references and what to adapt from each.
- `agency-websites-unverified.csv` - candidates that could not be verified in this session (see "Verification" below).
- `RESEARCH-REPORT.md` - this document.

## How the research was done

1. A long list of about 420 agencies was assembled from Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, FWA, The Brand Identity, SiteInspire, Godly, Land-book, Behance and prior knowledge of award-winning studios, then pared to 315 design-led candidates.
2. Every candidate was checked with a domain-restricted web search (results limited to the agency's own domain). A site counted as verified only when its own domain returned live, indexed pages describing that agency. This is the only verification method the research environment allowed: direct page fetching (curl, headless browser) is blocked by the environment's network policy, so the sites were not screenshotted here.
3. Verified sites were then annotated with design observations (hero, scroll, typography, portfolio, interaction) and rated for relevance to a premium ScaleAcres homepage. The design observations are the researcher's assessment; the "from search" columns in the CSV are the factual evidence (page titles, self-descriptions, clients, awards and tech hints that surfaced in results).
4. Entities that turned out to be something else (for example, kinetic.ae is a recruitment firm; augeweb.com is a one-person developer page) were dropped. Studios that are closing or merged (Franklyn, Animade, Heydays) and a domain hosting spam pages (Alphabetical) were excluded.

## Verification caveat (please read)

- **150 sites are in the master list, all verified live.** 14 further verified sites were trimmed to keep the list at 150; they are listed in the unverified CSV with status "VERIFIED (not in top 150)".
- **138 candidates were not checked** because the session's web-search allowance ran out mid-way. They are listed with their candidate URLs in `agency-websites-unverified.csv` and are NOT counted. Several are well-known award winners (Locomotive, Obys, Exo Ape, Immersive Garden, Resn, Instrument, Huge, Oddfellows, Ordinary Folk, ManvsMachine, Neoscape, Binyan, Significa, Koto, Kurppa Hosk, Uncommon, UNIT9, Universal Everything, North Kingdom, Mutabor, Resn, Foreign Policy Design, Schbang and others). A follow-up message will let the verification finish; the budget resets per message.
- **8 candidates returned zero results** from their domain (84.Paris, Build, Gunner, Here Design, Hornet, Ranger & Fox, Stept Studios, Yukfoo). They may block indexing or have moved; treat as unconfirmed.
- URLs in the master list are the homepage of the domain that was verified. Where the search surfaced a localised or sub-page URL (for example `/en/` or `/about`), the homepage root is given and the indexed URL is kept in the CSV for reference.

## Assumption about ScaleAcres

Relevance ratings assume ScaleAcres is positioning as a premium creative / marketing partner with a real-estate and property-development emphasis (the brief asks specifically for premium real-estate marketing references). "High" means the site's tone, structure or sector maps directly onto that positioning; "Medium" means strong craft worth studying for one or two patterns; "Low" means included for category completeness (for example pure film-production grids).

## Patterns worth carrying into the ScaleAcres direction

- **One signature hero moment, not many.** The strongest sites (Lusion, Akaru, The Boundary, BASIC/DEPT) commit to a single full-bleed video or real-time canvas with one line of copy, then get out of the way.
- **Smooth scroll is table stakes at this level.** Lenis / Locomotive-style inertia plus GSAP ScrollTrigger reveals appear on most High-relevance sites; the differentiator is restraint and pacing, not quantity of effects.
- **Editorial typography carries premium feel.** Oversized headlines with small, precise captions (COLLINS, Porto Rocha, Pentagram, Adoratorio) read as expensive without heavy decoration.
- **Property sites win on the case-study spine.** Hoyne, DBOX and Williams New York structure every project as place brand -> campaign assets -> launch outcome, which is the format a ScaleAcres portfolio should adopt.
- **Video belongs in the grid, not only the hero.** Hover-to-play tiles (BASIC/DEPT, Zajno, Visualhouse, Tendril) let film-led work sell itself on the homepage.
- **Services sections work when each service is proven by media.** The Boundary, Cuberto and Clay show the capability rather than list it.
- **Footers are short.** The premium pattern is a single large CTA, contact details, social links and legal, in that order.
