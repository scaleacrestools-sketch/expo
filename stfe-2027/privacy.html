Privacy notice

How your enquiry details are used

This website is operated for STFE 2027 — Smart Trade Fair & Expo — by the organiser, Smart Vision Event & Promotions.

What we collect

  • The details you enter in the exhibitor enquiry, callback or visitor registration forms (for example name, company, mobile number, email, city, stall preference, category and message).
  • Basic campaign information attached to your submission: the page you landed on, the referring website and any UTM campaign parameters in the link you followed.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry about exhibiting, to call you back, or to record your visitor registration.
  • To contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp about STFE 2027, as you agreed when submitting the form.
  • To understand which campaigns bring enquiries, so the organiser can improve how the event is promoted.

Sharing

Your details are handled by the organiser and the service providers that host this website and its forms. They are not sold. They are not published.
