FAQ

Before you enquire

What is STFE 2027?+
STFE — Smart Trade Fair & Expo — is a three-day business exhibition for entrepreneurs, startups and MSMEs to showcase their brands, meet the right people and learn about market trends and government policy.
Where and when is it happening?+
15–17 January 2027 at Soor Sadan Auditorium, near Nagar Nigam Office, M.G. Road, Sanjay Place, Agra (U.P.). Venue and directions.
Who can exhibit?+
Businesses in any of the 17 exhibitor categories, including a dedicated Startups & MSMEs category.
What is the stall cost?+
₹7,670 per sq. m including GST. A 9 sq. m stall — 3 × 3 m or 2 × 4.5 m — costs ₹69,030 including GST.
Does the cost include GST?+
Yes. ₹69,030 already includes GST. GST is not added again.
What is included in the stall?+
Octonorm stall; 1 table and 2 chairs; 2 lights and a charging point; fascia branding; lunch and tea arranged; and access to a B2B and B2C audience.
Which stall sizes are available?+
2 × 4.5 m (stated as the minimum size in the brochure) and 3 × 3 m. Both are 9 sq. m and ₹69,030 including GST.
Can I view the floor plan?+
Yes. The layout viewer shows the ground and first floor plans from the brochure. The plans are schematic and not to scale.
How do I enquire about availability?+
Use the exhibitor enquiry form, request a callback, WhatsApp, or call +91 96341 26260.
Is a stall confirmed after submitting the enquiry?+
No. Availability and allocation are subject to organiser confirmation. A stall is confirmed only after the organiser confirms it in writing.
Is visitor entry free?+
Yes — free entry, registration mandatory. Register here.
How can I contact the organiser?+
Smart Vision Event & Promotions — +91 96341 26260, svepindia@gmail.com, www.svepindia.com.
