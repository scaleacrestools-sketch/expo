About the Expo

A business platform built for entrepreneurs, startups and MSMEs.

STFE — Smart Trade Fair & Expo — is a three-day business exhibition in Agra where growing brands present their products and services to buyers, trade visitors and the wider business community.

The expo is positioned as a platform to showcase your brand in a professional exhibition setting, to meet the right people — prospective buyers, distributors, collaborators and fellow founders — and to learn about market trends and government policy relevant to small and medium enterprises.

Participation is open to businesses across 17 exhibitor categories, from manufacturing and consumer brands to services, education and financial institutions. Visitor entry is free with mandatory registration, giving exhibitors access to both a B2B and a B2C audience.

  • 01

    Showcase

    Present your products and services in a dedicated, branded exhibition stall over three days.

  • 02

    Connect

    Meet buyers, partners and decision-makers through B2B meetings and structured networking.

  • 03

    Learn

    Attend business sessions, workshops and expert conversations on market trends and policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions exhibitors ask before enquiring.

What is STFE 2027?
STFE — Smart Trade Fair & Expo — is a three-day business exhibition for entrepreneurs, startups and MSMEs. Exhibitors showcase their brands to trade and public visitors, take part in B2B meetings, and attend business sessions, workshops and expert conversations.
Where and when is it happening?
15–17 January 2027 at Soor Sadan Auditorium, near Nagar Nigam Office, M.G. Road, Sanjay Place, Agra, Uttar Pradesh. See venue and directions.
Who can exhibit?
Businesses in any of the 17 exhibitor categories — from fashion, electronics and food brands to financial services, education, renewable energy and consultants. Startups and MSMEs have a dedicated category. View all categories.
What is the stall cost?
₹7,670 per square metre including GST. A 9 sq. m stall — either 3 × 3 metres or 2 × 4.5 metres — is ₹69,030 including GST.
Does the cost include GST?
Yes. All prices shown on this website include GST. GST is not added again on top of ₹69,030.
What is included in the stall?
An Octonorm stall, 1 table and 2 chairs, 2 lights and a charging point, fascia branding, lunch and tea arranged, and access to a B2B and B2C audience.
Which stall sizes are available?
Two configurations, both 9 sq. m: Option A is 3 × 3 metres and Option B is 2 × 4.5 metres. Both are priced at ₹69,030 including GST. For other requirements, select “Need Guidance” in the enquiry form.
Can I view the floor plan?
Yes — the floor plan viewer shows the ground and first floor schematic layouts with zoom and an enlarged view. Layouts are schematic and not to scale.
How do I enquire about availability?
Submit the exhibitor enquiry form, request a callback, message the organiser on WhatsApp, or call +91 96341 26260. The organiser will confirm current availability and placement options.
Is a stall confirmed after submitting the enquiry?
No. An enquiry registers your interest. Stall availability and allocation are subject to organiser confirmation, and a stall is confirmed only once the organiser confirms it in writing and the booking process is completed.
Is visitor entry free?
Yes, entry is free for visitors. Registration is mandatory — register here.
How can I contact the organiser?
Smart Vision Event & Promotions — phone +91 96341 26260, email svepindia@gmail.com, website www.svepindia.com.
