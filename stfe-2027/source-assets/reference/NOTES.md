# UPITS reference: visual design notes (internal comparison only, not for publication)

These are captures of https://upinternationaltradeshow.com/ (with INDEX and MIPIM first screens for comparison), taken 2026-11-25 at 1440×900 and 390×844.

- **Header (three tiers):** (1) a thin purple-to-violet gradient utility bar with a pill-shaped email link on the left and a "UPITS Connect" button plus square social icons on the right; (2) a white logo strip with the logo lock-up and edition date badge ("25th–29th Sept 2026 · 4th Edition · venue") on the left and an orange-to-lilac gradient CTA tile ("Download Your Event Photos") on the right; (3) a full-width magenta/plum gradient nav bar with a home icon, uppercase condensed labels, and "+" dropdown indicators.
- **Hero:** an edge-to-edge autoplay video/banner with no text overlay. Government seals and dignitary cut-outs are pinned in the corners, and an orange rule sits under it. A chat widget floats bottom-right.
- **Stats band:** right under the hero, a rounded gradient (navy→plum) pill with four big numbers separated by thin dividers (Exhibitors / B2B Visitors / B2C Visitors / Business Enquiries).
- **Headings:** centred, uppercase, condensed bold (Rajdhani-like display font). The heading is two-tone, with navy words followed by magenta/orange accent words ("UPITS 2026: **SIX NATIONS. ONE GLOBAL PLATFORM.**"). Body text is a geometric sans (Poppins-like).
- **Palette:** deep navy/indigo (#2e2a5c-ish), magenta/plum (#8a1e6e-ish), saffron orange (#f26b21-ish) accents, white, and pale lilac/pink tinted section backgrounds. Gradients appear everywhere: purple→magenta and orange→lilac.
- **Section rhythm:** white and pale-lilac backgrounds alternate. Decorative Indian ethnic border strips (a repeating motif band) divide major sections. Content is boxed at a ~1140 px container, with generous 80–100 px vertical padding.
- **Cards:** rounded (~12 px) white cards with a thin orange outline and soft shadow, used for partner countries (flag + logo + landmark photo), dignitary quotes, partner logos, and past exhibitors. Many card rows are horizontal carousels with orange chevrons outside the container.
- **Photo usage:** real event photography in "Event at a Glance" cards (fashion show, laser show, exhibition stalls), with the photo on top and the title and caption below. Large portrait photography appears in a split block (photo left, dark navy topo-pattern panel right with quote and orange name pill). There are also video thumbnails for the films and highlights.
- **Video/gallery:** a "Highlights" section has a large featured video on the left and a vertical list of day-wise highlight videos on the right, inside a rounded outlined frame on a lilac band.
- **Info tiles:** four gradient (purple→plum) rounded tiles with icons (When / Where / Business Hours / Public Hours), plus a red "Updated News" ticker bar above them.
- **Lists:** two-column checklists with small orange hand/arrow icons inside a dashed-border box ("What makes UP…"). The Focus Sectors section has three columns, each with a purple header tab and circle-bullet lists, over a faint background image.
- **Exhibitors / Visitors split:** a full-width two-panel block, purple gradient (Exhibitors) beside orange gradient (Visitors), each with white two-column lists separated by thin rules.
- **CTAs:** pill buttons with gradient fills ("Know More", "Download Your Event Photos"). The primary CTA lives in the logo strip, not the hero. Secondary contact CTAs float as dark navy cards (Visitor Relations / Press & Media) overlapping the footer edge.
- **Partners:** sorted into labelled groups (Supporting Countries, Media, Marcom, Lucky Gift) as centred rows of white logo tiles with orange outlines. "Our Past Exhibitors" is a three-row auto-scrolling logo marquee.
- **Footer:** light background with columns (Organised By seals + visitor counter + socials, Exhibitors links, Visitors links, Relations/contacts/address, QR code + app store badges), then an illustrated skyline/monument strip in orange→purple gradient, and a thin purple copyright bar with legal links.
- **Mobile (390 px):** the header becomes the stacked logo/date lock-up plus a square grid-icon menu button. The hero video stays 16:9, the stats band overlaps the bottom of the hero in a single row of four, headings wrap to two-tone multi-line blocks, and partner cards remain a swipe carousel.
- **Comparison:** INDEX (Dubai, DWTC) uses a dark, minimal header: logo, date/venue, co-located show logos, and two outlined CTAs (Book a Stand / Remind Me to Attend) in the header. Its hero is a full-bleed black motion graphic with no text. MIPIM uses a dark teal hero with large condensed display type, a subtitle, a short paragraph, date/venue, and two CTAs (Register / Exhibit) on the left, plus an organic blob-masked photo on the right. Both are far more restrained than UPITS's dense, government-branded layering.
