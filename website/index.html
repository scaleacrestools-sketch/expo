Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on buying, paying and moving in with M3M. Still have a question? Our advisors are a call away.

Ask an advisor
Are all M3M projects RERA registered?

Yes. Every ongoing M3M project is registered with the respective state RERA (HRERA for Haryana, UPRERA for Uttar Pradesh). Registration numbers are published on each project page along with approved plans and quarterly progress reports.

What payment plans are available?

Most projects offer construction-linked plans, down-payment plans and, on select inventory, possession-linked or subvention schemes. Your advisor will share the plan matrix for the project you shortlist, including bank-loan tie-ups.

Can NRIs buy property with M3M?

Yes. NRIs and PIOs can purchase residential and commercial property in India under FEMA guidelines. Our NRI desk assists with documentation, NRE/NRO payments, power of attorney and home-loan sourcing.

How do I book a site visit?

Use the Enquire Now form, call 1800 123 3333 or message us on WhatsApp. Site visits run seven days a week from our sales galleries in Gurugram, Noida and Panipat, and we can arrange pick-up on request.

What is the typical possession timeline?

Possession dates are committed in the RERA registration and the agreement for sale. Construction updates are shared every quarter, and the customer portal shows milestone-wise progress for your tower.

Do you offer leasing or assured-return commercial products?

M3M Leasing manages retail and office spaces across delivered projects such as M3M Urbana, M3M Broadway and M3M IFC. Lease terms, lock-ins and fit-out support vary by asset; speak to the leasing desk for current availability.

What is Black Circle?

Black Circle is M3M's privilege programme for owners of branded residences, offering curated experiences, concierge services and access to partner luxury brands.
