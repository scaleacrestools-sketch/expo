About Scale Acres

Building the city we would want to live in

Scale Acres began with a single apartment block in Whitefield and a simple rule: design every home as if our own families would move in. Seventeen years later that rule still decides where we build, who we partner with, and how we hand over keys.

Every project is RERA-registered, delivered by an in-house execution team, and backed by a customer desk that stays with you long after possession.

Design firstArchitecture that gives you light, air and privacy before square footage.
On-time deliveryMilestone-linked construction with transparent progress updates.
Lasting valueLocations chosen for the next decade of growth, not the next quarter.
