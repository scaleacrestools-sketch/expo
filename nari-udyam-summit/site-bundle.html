21 FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Smart Vision Nari Udyam Summit & Expo?

Smart Vision Nari Udyam Summit & Expo is a premium 2-day business summit and expo dedicated to empowering women entrepreneurs through markets, money, mentors, networking, recognition and business growth opportunities.

Who can book a stall?

Women entrepreneurs, founders, creators, MSMEs, homepreneurs, product sellers, service providers, startups and women-led brands can book stalls.

Where will the event happen?

The event is planned in Delhi at a premium 5-star hotel.

What are the event dates?

The event is scheduled for 19–20 September 2026.

Is this only an exhibition?

No. It is a premium summit and expo that includes stalls, business sessions, networking, bank/MSME guidance, mentorship, awards and community collaboration opportunities.

Will exhibitors receive recognition?

Yes. Participating exhibitors will be honored with appreciation certificates during the event. Selected businesses will also be recognized through curated award categories.

Who will present the awards?

A respected Chief Guest to be Announced will be invited to present appreciation certificates and awards.

Is there a social cause connected with the event?

Yes. A percentage of every confirmed stall booking fee will be donated toward women empowerment initiatives through NGO partnerships. Details will be officially announced before the event.

Can banks and financial institutions participate?

Yes. Banks, NBFCs and financial institutions can participate as partners to guide eligible entrepreneurs on business finance, loan readiness, MSME support and growth opportunities.

How can I become a sponsor or partner?

You can submit the enquiry form or contact the Smart Vision team directly to request the partnership deck.

Are stalls limited?

Yes. The event will feature limited curated stalls to maintain quality, category balance and premium visibility.

How do I register my interest?

You can fill out the enquiry form on the website or connect with the Smart Vision team through WhatsApp.

Still have a question?

Connect with the Smart Vision team through WhatsApp or submit the enquiry form.
