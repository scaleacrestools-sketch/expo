# Brand tokens

Source: ScaleAcres Brand Kit v1.0. Its logo colours were sampled from the logo pixels on scaleacres.com. Re-sample from the master artwork when it is supplied.

| Token | Role | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-ink` | ScaleAcres Brand Black (wordmark) | `#0B0D0D` |
| `--color-blue` | ScaleAcres Brand Blue (symbol) | `#1CA6DC` |
| `--color-blue-deep` | Blue for text on white (AA at ≥ 18 px bold) and hover | `#1489B8` |
| `--color-white` | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` |
| `--color-offwhite` | Off-White section ground | `#F4F5F7` |
| `--color-grey-soft` | Soft Grey: supporting text on black, rules | `#C9CDD2` |
| `--color-grey` | Soft Grey: captions | `#9DA2A6` |
| `--color-grey-dark` | Dark Grey: body text on light | `#3A3F45` |
| `--color-ink-soft` | Graphite: raised surfaces on black | `#15181B` |

Coral, orange, red, yellow, pink and purple accents are not used.

Usage:
- Black sections carry white type with blue emphasis.
- White and off-white sections carry black type.
- The brand statement uses a full blue ground with black type.
- Buttons are white or black pills that turn blue on hover.
- Focus rings are blue.
