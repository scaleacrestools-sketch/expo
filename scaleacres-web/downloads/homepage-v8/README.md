# ScaleAcres homepage v8

**Positioning:** a real estate 360° marketing and creative agency, creative-first. The copy across the homepage, both new pages, the services list, the FAQ, the footer and the meta tags says so.

**Lead magnets:** four on the homepage, and the same ones on both new pages.
1. **Free 90-day launch plan:** the hero form.
2. **Free creative concept:** a new band after Services, offering one campaign idea with a key visual direction and a headline.
3. **Free 90-day launch plan:** a new band after "How we work".
4. **Free marketing audit:** the section before the contact card.

**Contact section:** this replaces the old "Let's work together" block, following the reference design. It has:
- a "Start a project" chip;
- the headline "Let's talk about your next launch.";
- both phone numbers;
- a white "Tell us about your project" form (name, phone, company or project, "You are a") with a "Book a strategy call" gradient button;
- a slim info bar with the address, "Offices in Agra and Delhi" and the email.

The main footer stays below it.

## Type (option 3, approved)
- **Clash Display** for every headline.
- **Satoshi** for text, buttons and the form.
- Both come from Fontshare under the ITF Free Font License, which allows commercial use.
- The text is unchanged. The sizes are re-tuned for the new type, and the hero headline sits on three lines: "Real Estate Marketing / That Builds / Real Demand."
- Highlighted words use colour instead of the old italic serif: Royal on light sections, Sky on dark ones.

## Services section (heading animation in the Red Dash Media style)
- **Left column:** text only: "What we do", the headline and the description, with no video. It stays pinned while the services list scrolls beside it.
- **Scroll motion:** the headline rises line by line out of its mask, then the description slides up and sharpens from a soft blur. The motion follows the scroll position and runs in reverse when you scroll back up.
- **Right column:** the services list is unchanged, with the same eight click-to-open items.
- **Text:** unchanged.
