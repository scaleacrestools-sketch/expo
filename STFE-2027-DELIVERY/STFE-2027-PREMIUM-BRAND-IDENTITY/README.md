## Editability: what is vector and what is not
- **Editable vector:** all headings and body copy (live `<text>` in Rajdhani and Manrope), colour blocks, gradients, lines, borders, icons, the Portal motif, momentum bands, the ornamental border, the cube texture (an SVG `<pattern>`), the Agra business skyline, and every mockup. That covers the gate, stall, signage, badges, brochure, invitation, letterhead, folder, social posts, banner, and the phone and browser frames.
- **Raster, embedded unchanged:**
  - **The approved STFE 2027 logo.** The only source supplied is a PNG (1379 × 649), so it is placed exactly as supplied. It has not been vector-traced, because an automatic trace of the multicolour swoosh and globe would alter the approved artwork. Replace it with the original vector logo if one exists.
  - **Photographs and website screenshots.** Each is a single, replaceable `<image>`.
- **Layers:** every SVG has named top-level layers (`Background`, `Artwork`, `Folio`) and meaningful IDs (`portal-motif-…`, `badge-exhibitor-…`, `ornament-border-…`, `approved-logo-…`).
- **Shadows:** these use standard SVG drop-shadow filters. Illustrator and Inkscape keep them. Figma may import them as effects or drop them.

## Honesty notes
- **Conceptual work:** the entrance gate, hanging banners, stall fascia, signage, badges, print pieces and digital ads are design concepts. They are labelled as concepts and were not produced or built.
- **Placeholders:** names, companies, stall numbers, QR codes and directions are placeholders. No real attendees or allocations are shown.
- **Photography:** the images are illustrative visuals supplied by the organiser. They are not photographs of a previous STFE edition.
- **Government marks:** the MSME, Make in India and Digital India marks are not used anywhere in this identity package. They may only appear with written authorisation and approved relationship wording.
- **Facts:** dates, venue, the price (₹69,030 including GST, ₹7,670 per m²), the 3 × 3 m and 2 × 4.5 m stall sizes and the contact details come from the official brochure.
