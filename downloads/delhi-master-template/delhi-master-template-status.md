## How the page is composed

Each section uses the live Delhi page's own content:

1. **Dark hero.** The original H1 is set at display size, with a label and breadcrumb. The page's own Delhi NCR image sits behind it, and the live enquiry form is in a glass card.
2. **Client logo wall.** Equal greyscale tiles, no oversized logos.
3. **Client stories.** A rail of founder cards: photo, name, company.
4. **Statement.** "Converting Curious Visitors…" with the 1500+ figure.
5. **Branding showcase.** One lead image, two beside it, and rows that fill edge to edge.
6. **Noida split.** Project cards in a dark panel.
7. **Funnel.** Three stage cards with the live images and lists.
8. **Results.** Campaign screenshots in browser frames.
9. **Website portfolio, curated.**
   - Two lead projects large, four more beneath.
   - The other 13 sit in a "More websites" gallery rail with previous/next controls. Every project stays on the page and crawlable.
   - Screenshots sit in fixed frames and scroll on hover, so there are no full-length dumps.
10. **"Our Successful Delhi Projects"** logo collage.
11. **Awards & Recognitions** split.
12. **Culture.** Photos in their natural proportions.
13. **"Grow Faster…"** editorial split.
14. **"Why Work with Us".** Numbered rows on black.
15. **"Why Digital Marketing… Delhi NCR"** mirrored split.
16. **"Our Creative Magic"** creatives strip.
17. **FAQ.** Two columns; all answers are in the page.
18. **Closing CTA and black footer.**
