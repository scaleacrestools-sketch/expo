03 · Logo versions

One logo, several fits

Use the primary logo wherever you can. Choose another version only when the space, background or production method needs it. Never redraw or rearrange the logo yourself.

04 · Clear space & minimum size

Room to breathe

X is the cap height of SCALEACRES. Keep at least half of X clear on every side of the logo, free of text, edges and other graphics. More space is always better.

05 · Logo on backgrounds

Always on solid ground

Place the logo on brand colours or calm, even areas of photography. Pick the version that keeps the strongest contrast: full colour on light, reversed on dark, white on royal and on images.

06 · Logo misuse

Please don't

The logo only works if it looks the same every time. Use the supplied files exactly as they are. If a layout seems to need any of these changes, choose a different logo version instead.

08 · Typography

Outfit, one family

Outfit is the brand typeface for headlines, body and UI. It is free on Google Fonts. Headlines are set heavy, uppercase and tight; body text is regular, sentence case and generous. The logo's lettering is artwork: never retype it.

10 · Imagery

Real work, real places

Our pictures show architecture, the people who make the work, and the work itself. Grade images cool and clean, with deep shadows and crisp whites. That way photography sits naturally next to Royal and Black.

ArchitectureProjects at blue hour, strong verticals, clean geometry. Echoes the rising symbol.
Studio & teamReal people at work: screens, shoots, strategy sessions. Natural, unposed.
The workWebsites, ads and films shown in context, on devices and at full quality.
Data & growthDashboards and results, simplified and graded into the brand palette.

Do

  • Use ScaleAcres' own projects, clients' approved creatives and real team photography.
  • Keep images sharp, high resolution and in their original aspect ratio.
  • Leave a calm area for the logo and headline.

Avoid

  • Generic stock: handshakes, pointing at whiteboards, staged laughter.
  • Warm sepia filters, heavy HDR and oversaturated skies.
  • Fake mock-ups of work that does not exist.

11 · Voice & tone

Confident, clear, measurable

We sound like a senior partner who has done this many times: direct, warm and specific. Short lines. Real outcomes. No jargon that a property developer or a founder would have to look up.

Confident, not loudState what we do and what it achieves. Let results carry the weight.
Clear, not cleverPlain words over buzzwords. One idea per sentence.
SystematicExplain the process: how we plan, build, launch and improve.
