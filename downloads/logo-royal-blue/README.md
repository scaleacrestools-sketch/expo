# ScaleAcres — Royal Blue logo explorations

**Proposals for review. These are not approved artwork.** The official logo is unchanged and still in use.

These keep the official logo's structure, proportions and lettering. Only the colour changes. They were traced from the supplied 2000 px logo; the traced shapes match the original to within 0.4% of pixels.

## Files (for each variant `NN-scaleacres-<name>`)
- `.svg`: vector, transparent background, with gradients. Use this for the web, print and scaling.
- `.png`: transparent at 2× (≈ 2990 px wide).
- `-on-background.svg` / `.png`: shown on its recommended background.
- `-symbol.svg` / `.png`: the building mark on its own (icons, avatars, favicons; PNG at 4×).
- `scaleacres-royal-blue-logo-sheet.png`: every variant on one sheet.

## Variants
"Wordmark" means SCALEACRES plus the left half of the building. "Symbol" means the right half of the building.

Symbol gradients rise from the base of the building to its top, from deep to light, as a nod to "scaling". Strapline gradients run left to right. Royal Sweep runs one gradient across the whole lockup.

## Recommendation
- **Primary logo: 02 Royal Gradient.** The black wordmark keeps the logo's authority and legibility. The rising royal gradient makes the symbol feel modern and "scaling up". It is also the smallest step from today's logo.
- **On dark backgrounds and video:** 08 Electric on Dark.
- **On royal backgrounds:** 09 Reversed on Royal, or 12 White Knockout.
- **Single-colour print, embroidery and stamps:** 11 One-Colour Royal.
- **Luxury real estate campaigns:** 07 Royal × Gold, as an alternative.
- **Use with care:** 05 Royal Sweep and 06 Royal × Violet are the most expressive. Keep them for campaigns rather than the core logo.

## Production note
The SVGs are high-quality traces of the supplied raster logo. They are not the original vector master. For large-format print, apply the same colours and gradients to the original vector file (AI, EPS or SVG) if you have it.
