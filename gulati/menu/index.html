Restaurant Menu

The menu is a journey of over 200 recipes — from signature classics to indulgent kebabs and regional treasures. Every dish is crafted to delight, impress, and transport guests into a world of authentic Indian flavors. The elaborate Buffet Lunch, featuring 25+ daily-changing dishes, offers a dynamic and ever-evolving culinary experience.

A Taste of Tradition

Our curated menu brings together the richness of North Indian and Mughlai cuisine, from slow-cooked curries and clay-oven breads to fragrant Dum Pukht biryanis and perfectly grilled kebabs. Each dish reflects a deep respect for culinary heritage, balanced with precision and an unwavering commitment to quality.

The experience concludes with an indulgent selection of traditional Indian desserts, including gulab jamun, kheer and halwa. A considered beverage menu complements the meal, completing an experience that celebrates the depth, warmth and enduring appeal of Indian cuisine.
