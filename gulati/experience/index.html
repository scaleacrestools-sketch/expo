The Experience

Two rooms, one welcome. At Pandara Road, the address where it all began in 1959, the booths are red velvet, the floor is chequered marble and the walls carry six decades of photographs. At DT Mega Mall in Gurugram, the same legacy takes an elegant, contemporary form under chandeliers and arches. In both, the service is attentive, the food is authentic and every visit feels like a return.

Pandara Road, New Delhi

The original. A humble dhaba with wooden benches grew into a landmark of Indian culinary culture, and the flavours have remained unchanged. Lunch is served every day from 12pm to 4pm, dinner from 7pm until late.

DT Mega Mall, Gurugram

Golf Course Road, Sector 28. The newer address, where the same spirit takes contemporary form. Ideal for a family lunch, a quiet dinner or a celebration with those you love most. Private events are welcome for both lunch and dinner, seven days a week.
