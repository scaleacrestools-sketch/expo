Our Story

Our Story

Since 1959, Gulati has stood at Pandara Road as Delhi's most enduring and beloved dining address. What began as a humble dhaba with wooden benches and soulful, unpretentious meals has grown into something far greater — a landmark of Indian culinary culture. Over six decades, Gulati has served presidents and poets, families and first dates, locals and travellers from across the world. The flavours have remained unchanged. The warmth has never wavered. This is not just a restaurant. It is a living institution — one that Delhi has claimed as its own, and the world has come to admire.

The Craft

The Gulati kitchen runs on memory as much as mastery. Recipes handed down through decades. Techniques refined over thousands of repetitions. Flavours calibrated to a standard that has not shifted in over sixty-five years. The Dal Makhani is still slow-cooked on a low flame through the night. The Butter Chicken is still prepared with the same careful balance of spice and richness that made it legendary. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is compromised.

The Invitation

Gulati now welcomes you at two addresses — our original home at Pandara Road, New Delhi, where the story began in 1959, and our newer location in Gurugram at Mega Mall, Sector 28, where the same legacy has taken elegant contemporary form. Both restaurants carry the same spirit: attentive service, authentic food, and an atmosphere that makes every visit feel like a return. Whether you are joining us for a family lunch, a quiet dinner, or a celebration with those you love most, we are ready for you. At Gulati, every guest becomes part of a story worth telling.

The Milestones

More than three decades ago, Gulati introduced the Navratra Food Festival, a celebration of pure vegetarian cuisine prepared without onion or garlic, rooted in tradition and respect for the devotion of its guests. It marked the beginning of a legacy of culinary firsts.

Gulati went on to introduce Mutton Chole Bhature, giving a beloved classic an unexpected new expression that went on to become iconic across Delhi NCR. It created Parda Chicken Biryani, Edamame Galouti and an array of signature kebabs, each pushing the boundaries of Indian cuisine while remaining firmly rooted in its traditions.
