The Legacy

Since 1959, Gulati has been Delhi's most cherished dining address, a place where extraordinary food meets genuine warmth, and where six decades of craft arrive at your table in every single bite. Born on Pandara Road as a humble dhaba, we have grown into a globally recognised institution, counted among the 100 Most Legendary Restaurants in the World. Yet the spirit has never changed. The welcome is still the welcome. This is Gulati: Delhi's own, and the world's favourite.
